Career Launch Pad — CV Writing & Interview Preparation

📅 20th March 2026
📍 Batticaloa, Sri Lanka
🎓 Undergraduate Students
👤 Resource Person: M.L. Irsath Ali

About This Event

Invited as Resource Person by the Faculty of Commerce & Management and the Career Guidance Cell at Eastern University of Sri Lanka, I delivered a practical workshop on CV writing best practices and interview preparation for the 2022/2023 undergraduate batch.

The session focused on equipping students with the real-world skills needed to enter the competitive job market — covering ATS-friendly CV structures, professional presentation, and interview confidence strategies drawn from industry experience.

Session Highlights

  • CV writing best practices aligned with modern ATS systems
  • Interview preparation strategies from industry perspective
  • Career readiness guidance for 2022/2023 undergraduate batch
  • Practical Q&A session with students
  • Physical session at Faculty of Commerce & Management, Batticaloa
