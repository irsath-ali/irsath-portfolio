Data Storm Masterclass 2.0 — The Business of Analytics

📅 2nd June 2026

About This Event

Conducted Data Storm Masterclass 2.0 — The Business of Analytics as part of Data Storm v7.0 — Sri Lanka's premier advanced analytics competition, organised by the Rotaract Clubs of University of Moratuwa and Faculty of Science, University of Colombo. Powered by Octave.

The session focused on the business dimension of analytics — how data science creates real commercial value, and how to think like both a data scientist and a business strategist when solving real-world problems.

Session Highlights

  • The business of analytics — bridging data science and commercial value
  • Real-world case studies from enterprise analytics at Sysco Labs
  • How to think like a data scientist and a business strategist simultaneously
  • Part of Data Storm v7.0 — Sri Lanka's premier analytics competition
  • Powered by Octave — John Keells Group
