About This Workshop

A 2-day industry-oriented Data Science workshop delivered to 2nd year Data Science students at NIBM Sri Lanka — in collaboration with Sysco Labs. The workshop was designed to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world industry practice, giving students hands-on exposure to production-grade ML workflows used in enterprise environments.

Students worked through an end-to-end Retail Demand Forecasting pipeline — from data preparation and feature engineering to model building, evaluation, and deployment-ready folder structures — mirroring how ML projects are actually built and shipped in industry.

Topics Covered

  • Hands-on end-to-end ML solution — Retail Demand Forecasting pipeline from scratch
  • Production-ready folder structure — how real ML projects are organised in industry
  • Feature optimised ADS Testing — feature engineering and selection best practices
  • Git & Model Monitoring — version control and production monitoring for ML systems
  • CV & Interview Preparation — how to position yourself for data science roles in industry
  • Live coding and Q&A sessions with real industry practitioner
