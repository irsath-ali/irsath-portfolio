The Future in Data & AI — Unlocking Data Science

📅 19 October 2024, 7:00 PM
💻 Via Zoom
🎓 School Leavers & Undergraduates
👤 Resource Person: M.L. Irsath Ali

About This Event

Invited by the Undergraduates Association Akkaraipattu (UGAA) to deliver an inspiring talk on the future of Data & AI — aimed at school leavers and undergraduates from the Eastern Province considering careers in data science and technology.

As someone who grew up in Akkaraipattu and went on to build a career in enterprise AI, this session held special significance — directly inspiring the next generation of data professionals from the same community.

Session Highlights

  • The future landscape of Data Science and AI careers
  • How to break into data science from Sri Lanka
  • Real career journey — from Akkaraipattu to Fortune 500
  • Guidance for school leavers and undergraduates on career paths
  • Organised by Undergraduates Association Akkaraipattu (UGAA)
