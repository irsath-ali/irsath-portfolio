Building a Winning CV — Expert Tips for Effective CV Writing & Interview Success

About This Event

Delivered an in-depth 2-hour online workshop for Stat Circle, University of Colombo on building winning CVs and succeeding in job interviews — aimed at statistics and data science students preparing to enter the competitive job market.

The session combined practical CV writing frameworks with real interview preparation strategies, drawing on industry hiring experience across banking, tech, and Fortune 500 enterprise environments.

Session Highlights

  • Expert tips for effective CV writing that stands out to recruiters
  • Interview preparation — confidence, structure, and delivery
  • Real examples of winning CVs from data science and analytics roles
  • Live Q&A — 2-hour interactive session
  • Organised by Stat Circle, University of Colombo
