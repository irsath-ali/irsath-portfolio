CV Crafting Workshop — Chapter 01

📅 27th May 2026, 7:00 PM

About This Event

Invited by Stat Circle, University of Colombo — under their ASCENIX initiative — to deliver a CV Crafting Workshop for statistics and data science students at the University of Colombo.

The session focused on building CVs that stand out in competitive data and analytics job markets — drawing on real hiring experience from Sysco Labs and the John Keells Group.

Session Highlights

  • CV structure and design for data science and analytics roles
  • How to highlight academic projects and internships effectively
  • ATS optimisation for modern job applications
  • Real examples and live feedback on student CVs
  • Organised by Stat Circle, University of Colombo — ASCENIX Chapter 01
