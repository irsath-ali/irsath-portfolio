CV Writing & Cover Letter Preparation Workshop

📍 Eastern University · Batticaloa
🎓 Undergraduate Students
👤 Resource Person: M.L. Irsath Ali

About This Event

Workshop delivered to undergraduate students at the Faculty of Agriculture, Eastern University of Sri Lanka — covering professional CV writing, cover letter strategy, and career readiness for the modern job market.

Students were guided through practical frameworks for building strong CVs and cover letters that stand out to employers — drawing on real industry hiring experience across Sri Lanka and international markets.

Session Highlights

  • Professional CV structure and formatting best practices
  • Cover letter writing strategy tailored to job applications
  • Career readiness guidance for agriculture graduates entering the job market
  • Interactive session with large undergraduate audience
