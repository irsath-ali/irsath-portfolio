About Me

Practitioner. Educator.
Community Builder.

I'm a Senior Data & AI Scientist at Sysco Labs (part of Sysco Corporation — the world's largest food distribution company), where I build ML systems for demand forecasting, out-of-stock pattern recognition, and supply chain optimisation at enterprise scale.

Beyond my corporate work, I'm deeply committed to democratising AI education in Sri Lanka. As a Visiting Lecturer at NIBM and IIT Sri Lanka, and through NexAI — my AI-native education and research platform — I deliver hands-on training that bridges the gap between theory and real industry practice.

I'm based in Akkaraipattu, Eastern Province, with roots in Colombo — and I work with institutions and organisations across Sri Lanka and the Gulf.
