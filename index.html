About Me

Practitioner. Educator.
Community Builder.

I'm a Senior Data & AI Scientist at Sysco Labs — part of Sysco Corporation, the world's largest food distribution company — where I build production-grade machine learning systems for demand forecasting, supply chain optimisation, and AI governance at enterprise scale.

Beyond my corporate work, I serve as a Visiting Lecturer at NIBM and IIT Sri Lanka, and I'm the Founder of NexAI — an AI-native education and research platform built to make practical data science accessible across Sri Lanka and the Gulf.

With over 10 years across banking, insurance, and enterprise tech, I bring real-world experience into every classroom, workshop, and training session I deliver.
