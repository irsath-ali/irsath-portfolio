About Me

Data & AI Practitioner.
Educator. Community Builder.

I'm a Senior Data & AI Scientist at Sysco Labs — part of Sysco Corporation, the world's largest food distribution company — where I build production-grade ML systems for demand forecasting, supply chain optimisation, and AI governance at enterprise scale.

With over 10 years across banking, insurance, consulting, and enterprise tech, I bring real industry experience into every classroom, workshop, and training session.
